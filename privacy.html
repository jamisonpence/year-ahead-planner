Privacy Policy

Last updated: 20 August 2026

The short version. MyLifos stores what you put into it. That includes some genuinely sensitive things — health metrics, medications, food logs, journal entries, finances, faith practices, political views, and notes about your family.

We do not sell your data, we do not use it for advertising, and we do not use it to train AI models. Nothing you write is visible to another person unless you explicitly share it. You can export everything or delete your account, permanently, from Settings at any time.

This policy explains what MyLifos ("we", "us") collects, why, and what happens to it. It applies to the website at mylifos.com and to the MyLifos iOS app.

Information you give us

Account

Your name, email address, and profile picture, from Sign in with Apple, Google Sign-In, or an email and password you choose. If you use Sign in with Apple and select "Hide My Email", we only ever receive Apple's relay address. We never see your Apple or Google password.

What you track

MyLifos is a personal life-management app, so most of what it holds is content you typed in. Being specific about it matters more than a general phrase like "life tracking data":

PlanningGoals, key results, projects, tasks, habits, calendar events, journal entries, weekly reviews

Sensitive categories

Some of the above is treated as sensitive personal data under laws including the GDPR and UK GDPR: health and medication information, religious beliefs and practices, political opinions, and information about children. We hold it only because you chose to record it, we process it solely to provide the features you are using, and you can delete any of it at any time. If you would rather not store a particular category, simply do not use that section — no feature depends on another.

Information collected automatically

  • Session and authentication data — a login cookie on the web, or a signed access token on the iOS app.
  • Last-active timestamp — so we can tell how many people actively use MyLifos.
  • Push notification tokens — an identifier from Apple, only if you turn notifications on. It identifies the device, not you, and is deleted when you disable notifications or delete the app.
  • Server logs — request method, path, status and timing. We deliberately do not log response bodies, because those contain your personal content.

MyLifos contains no advertising, no analytics SDKs, and no third-party trackers.

AI features

Some optional features — meal and trip suggestions, weekly planning — use Anthropic's Claude API. These work only if you supply your own Anthropic API key in Settings; we do not provide one, and no AI feature runs without it. Your key is encrypted before storage.

When you use one of those features, the relevant content is sent to Anthropic under your own API account and is governed by your agreement with Anthropic. We do not send your data to Anthropic for any other purpose, and neither we nor Anthropic use it to train models.

Sharing

We do not sell, rent or trade your personal information, and we do not share it for advertising. It leaves your account only in these cases:

  • People you choose. Sharing is off by default and set per section. Some things are never shareable regardless of settings — your prayer list is one.
  • Service providers who run MyLifos on our behalf: our hosting and database provider, our image storage provider, and our transactional email provider. They process data only to provide those services.
  • Content lookups. When you search for a film, book, album, recipe, plant, sports event or scripture passage, we pass your search terms to the relevant provider (for example TMDB, Google Books, Last.fm, Ticketmaster or a scripture API) to fetch the result. We send the search terms only — never your name, account or any other personal data.
  • Legal requirements, or to protect our rights or someone's safety.

Optional integrations

Each of these is off until you connect it, and each can be disconnected from Settings:

  • Google Calendar — we store OAuth tokens to read and display your events. We never modify or delete anything in your Google Calendar. Disconnecting deletes the tokens and the synced events.
  • Google Contacts, LinkedIn and Facebook — if you import contacts, we store the details you import, which may include other people's names, email addresses, phone numbers and birthdays. Please only import contacts where you are comfortable doing so. Disconnecting removes the imported data.

Storage, security and location

Your data is stored in a PostgreSQL database hosted in the United States, and uploaded images are held in object storage. Traffic is encrypted in transit with TLS. Passwords are hashed, never stored in readable form, and stored API keys are encrypted with AES-256. If you use MyLifos from outside the United States, your data is transferred to and processed there.

No service can promise perfect security, and we will not pretend otherwise. What we can say is that we minimise what we collect, we do not log your content, and we do not share it for commercial purposes.

Retention, export and deletion

We keep your data for as long as your account exists. From Settings you can:

  • Export everything as a single JSON file — goals, tasks, health, journal, and every other section.
  • Delete your account, which permanently removes your personal data from our systems. This cannot be undone. Content you shared with another person may remain visible to them where it has become part of their own records.

Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access, correct, export, or delete your personal data, to object to or restrict its processing, and to withdraw consent. The export and delete tools in Settings cover most of this immediately; for anything else, contact us at the address below. If you are in the UK or EU you also have the right to complain to your data protection authority.

Children

MyLifos is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly allow anyone under 13 to create an account. The Kids section is a tool for a parent or guardian to record information about their own children; that information is entered by the adult account holder, is visible only to them unless they share it, and is deleted along with the account. If you believe a child has created an account, contact us and we will remove it.

Changes to this policy

If we change this policy we will update the date at the top of this page, and for significant changes we will notify you in the app.
