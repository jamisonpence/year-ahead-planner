Support

Help with MyLifos — mylifos.com and the iOS app.

Email jamisonpence@gmail.com

MyLifos is built and run by one person, so replies usually take a day or two. If something is broken or you've lost data, say so in the subject line and it goes to the front of the queue.

It helps to include: what you were doing, what you expected, what happened instead, and whether you were on the web or the iOS app.

Common questions

How do I delete my account?

In the app or on the web: Settings → Danger Zone → Delete account. This permanently removes your account and all the data in it. It cannot be undone, and you don't need to email anyone to do it.

Can I get my data out?

Yes. Settings → Export your data downloads everything — goals, tasks, health, journal, recipes, and every other section — as a single JSON file. No limits, no waiting, no need to ask.

Who can see what I write?

Nobody, unless you choose otherwise. Sharing is off by default and set per section, so you might share your reading list with a friend while everything else stays private. Your prayer list can't be shared at all, by design.

How do I stop seeing someone, or report something?

Tap the flag icon on any post, comment or message from another person. From there you can report the content or block its author. Blocking hides you from each other in both directions and stops any further messages or friend requests. Blocked accounts are listed in Settings → Blocked users, where you can undo it.

I'm not getting notifications

Check Settings → Push notifications is switched on. On iPhone, also check iOS Settings → MyLifos → Notifications, since iOS only asks once and a declined prompt can't be reopened from inside the app.

If it still doesn't work, Settings → Push diagnostics shows where in the chain it's failing. Sending me a photo of that panel tells me almost everything I need.

Why do the AI features ask for an API key?

Meal suggestions, trip planning and weekly planning use Anthropic's Claude. MyLifos doesn't ship an API key, so those features are inactive until you add your own in Settings. Your key is encrypted before it's stored, and requests go to Anthropic under your own account. Every other part of the app works without it.

Is my health and financial data safe?

It's stored on servers in the United States, encrypted in transit, and never sold, shared for advertising, or used to train AI models. The privacy policy sets out exactly what is collected and why, including the sensitive categories.

Something looks wrong after an update

Try reloading first — immediately after a release you can catch the changeover. If it persists, email me with a screenshot.

Reporting a security issue

If you've found a vulnerability, please email jamisonpence@gmail.com with "security" in the subject rather than posting it publicly. I'll acknowledge it and tell you what I'm doing about it.
