Recipes & meals

A cookbook that's already full

Most food apps hand you an empty recipe box. This one arrives stocked, with everything already measured and costed out nutritionally.

  • 1,600+ recipes across 31 categories — Chicken, Pasta, Seafood, Steak, Keto, Vegan, Whole30, Kid-Friendly, Bread Machine and more, 30 in each
  • Macros calculated from real ingredients, not guessed — quantities are parsed and costed properly, so the numbers mean something
  • Full ingredient lists and step-by-step method on every single recipe
  • Generate a week of meals around your macros — enter your stats and goal and it works out your calorie and protein targets, then builds a plan that hits them
  • Filtered to how you actually eat — vegan, vegetarian, keto, Whole30, Mediterranean, gluten-free or dairy-free, plus any ingredient you want excluded outright
  • Grocery lists build themselves from the week's plan, and can be shared with your household
  • Three ideas surface each morning on your Today page, drawn from the categories you actually cook

Health & training

Tell it your goal, get the plan

Name what you're chasing — a 230lb bench or a first half marathon — and the whole schedule gets written for you, week by week, session by session.

  • Strength plans built from your numbers — say you bench 200 and want 230, and it periodises the whole run: heavy and volume days, working percentages, accessory work, a peak, a deload, then a max test
  • Eight couch-to-race plans: 5K, 10K, half marathon, marathon, 50K, 50-mile, sprint triathlon and olympic triathlon
  • Every session is prescribed — sets, reps, working weights, easy runs, long runs, swim, bike and rest days already laid out
  • Exercise library organised by muscle group, equipment and category, so it adapts to a full gym or a set of dumbbells
  • Nutrition and vitals tracked alongside training, in the same place as your recipes
  • Log from a notification — one tap, without opening the app

Home

The jobs nobody remembers

Furnace filters, gutter cleaning, the dishwasher filter you've never once cleaned. Set the cadence once and stop holding it in your head.

  • Chores by category — Cleaning, Yard, Maintenance, Laundry, Cooking — with priority levels
  • Eight recurrence options: daily, weekly, fortnightly, monthly, quarterly, yearly, a custom number of days, or simply as-needed
  • Appliance upkeep tracked per appliance, so filter and service intervals aren't guesswork
  • Home projects broken into tasks, sitting alongside the recurring work rather than in a separate app
  • Missed chores reschedule themselves instead of stacking into a wall of red overdue items
  • Share with your household so the same list works for everyone in the house

Goals, habits & planning

From a five-year ambition down to this morning

Long-range goals usually die because nothing connects them to today. Here the chain is explicit: horizon, goal, project, task, today.

  • Four planning horizons — this year, next year, three-year and five-year — so ambitions don't all compete for the same twelve months
  • Goals break into projects, projects into tasks, and those tasks appear on your daily list
  • Habits with streak insurance — earn a freeze for consistency so one missed day doesn't erase two months of work
  • Put stakes on a goal by naming a friend who'll hold you to it
  • Weekly review that looks back at what moved and sets the week ahead
  • Evening close-out to tick off habits, jot one line, and pick tomorrow's first task

Hobbies & interests

The thing you keep meaning to get better at

Hobbies stall because "get better at guitar" isn't a plan. Pick what you're into and there's already a structured route from where you are to where you wanted to be.

  • 50 ready-made plans spanning chess, poker, hiking, climbing, cycling, running, surfing, fishing, gardening, birding, languages, instruments, singing, acting, comedy and dance
  • Every plan comes broken into steps with a sensible duration — four weeks to finish a piece, twelve to build a portfolio — so you always know the next move
  • Or start from a type — creative, collection, outdoor, games, learning or performance — and shape it to whatever you're doing
  • Track sessions and progress against the plan rather than guessing whether you're actually improving

People

Stop maintaining your friends' details by hand

Everyone keeps a private copy of the same birthday. When your friends are in here too, that stops being your job.

  • Friends' birthdays fill themselves in from their own profile, and land on your calendar
  • See where everyone is — friends grouped by city, so you know who to message before a trip
  • Per-field privacy — share your birthday without sharing your relationship status; anything private never leaves your account
  • Keep in touch reminders at whatever cadence each person deserves
  • Recommendations and messages in the same place as the person's profile
  • Family and partner links that both people have to confirm
